The chart deploys the app only — make sure Postgres (with the vector extension), Kafka,
Redis, Elasticsearch, and Keycloak are reachable at the addresses in values (config.*).

Check status:
  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

Port-forward and hit the API:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "task-manager.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Actuator is on the pod's management port, which the Service does not expose:
  kubectl port-forward deploy/{{ include "task-manager.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.managementPort }}:{{ .Values.managementPort }}
  curl http://localhost:{{ .Values.managementPort }}/actuator/health

Swagger UI:
  http://localhost:8080/swagger-ui/index.html
